Scaffold Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Scaffold is a small beta tool for educators planning lessons for multilingual learners.

Information we use

When you sign in with Google, Scaffold receives your verified Google account identifier and email address so it can authenticate your session and provide administrator access when your account is allowlisted. We do not ask for or collect student names or other student-identifying information.

Lesson generation

When you request a lesson, the planning information you submit is sent to OpenAI to generate the requested lesson. Scaffold does not use that information to build advertising profiles or sell it. Usage records contain hashed identifiers and operational counts, such as generation totals, limits, failures, and approximate token usage.

Storage and retention

Operational usage records are stored on the application server to enforce beta limits and protect service costs. Saved lessons in the browser remain under your control. We retain operational records only as needed to run, secure, and improve the beta.

Questions

For privacy questions or additional beta access, use the contact link shown in Scaffold or the project’s GitHub repository.
